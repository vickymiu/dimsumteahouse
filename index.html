Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains how information submitted through the Dim Sum Teahouse booking form is collected and used.

Information we collect

When you submit a table booking request, we collect the information you provide, such as your name, email address, phone number, preferred date and time, party size and any notes you add.

How we use your information

We use this information to review and respond to your booking request, contact you about the reservation, and handle relevant questions or special requests. Submitting a request does not mean your reservation is confirmed.

Form processing and retention

Your form is processed using FormSubmit, a third-party form service, to deliver your request to the restaurant. Please avoid including unnecessary sensitive personal information in the notes field. We keep booking information only for as long as reasonably needed to manage requests, maintain relevant business records and meet applicable legal obligations.

Your questions

If you have a question about this policy or the information you submitted, please contact the restaurant using the contact details on this website.

Before accepting live bookings, please make sure this policy reflects the restaurant's actual data handling and retention practices.
